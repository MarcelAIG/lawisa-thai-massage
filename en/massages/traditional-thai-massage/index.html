THE EXPERIENCE

Measured pressure and unhurried stretches

Moving thoughtfully from feet to shoulders, your practitioner alternates rhythmic palm and thumb acupressure with slow assisted stretches guided by your breath. Unlike warm-oil treatments built around fluid strokes on bare skin, traditional Thai massage is received clothed and oil-free to release deep muscular tension and restore natural ease of movement, at an intensity always tuned to your comfort.
